# AI agents in protein design

A participant’s account of the **Anthropic × Adaptyv Protein Design Competition**: five targets, structural hypotheses, and the decisions behind our designs.

My colleague and I entered the open track to explore a human-directed AI co-scientist workflow. Agents proposed hypotheses, performed analyses and reviewed evidence; we set priorities, challenged conclusions and made the final decisions. We submitted designs to all five challenges. Experimental performance is not reported here.
